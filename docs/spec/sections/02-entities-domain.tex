\section{Domain entities}
\label{sec:entities-domain}

Everything here exists in a barangay health center whether or not software is built. In
simulation these entities hold generated values, in deployment values from clinic records; they
are described in clinic terms, not algorithmic ones.

\noindent A filled square marks the entities built by the first version; the rest are defined for
a later one.

Barangay Health Center\core & A village health facility that keeps its own records and
  trains the model on them. & Counted per facility; identified by its national facility
  code and Philippine Standard Geographic Code (PSGC) where these are obtainable (OD-6). \\ \hline
\rowcolor{rowshade}Health Commodity\core & One medicine as stocked: generic
  name, dosage form, strength and unit. & Facilities counting a medicine in different
  units cannot be compared until converted. \\ \hline
Demand Observation\core & Units of one commodity given out at one facility in one period,
  with its source and completeness. & The model's only input. \\ \hline
\rowcolor{rowshade}Reporting Period\core & The span one observation covers (a day, a week
  or a month). & Totals and the surge window depend on it. \\ \hline
Surge\core & A rise in demand above a stated threshold over a stated window. & A definition,
  not a measurement: two definitions give two answers (Section~\ref{sec:open}). \\ \hline
\rowcolor{rowshade}Connectivity Episode & A span when a facility could not be reached,
  with cause and duration. & Whether a facility could be reached in a
  round is recorded with that round (Section~\ref{sec:entities-system}). \\ \hline
Forecast\core & Predicted units for one facility, one commodity and one future period,
  with horizon, uncertainty and model version. & Unusable without its model version. \\ \hline
\rowcolor{rowshade}Stock Position & Units on hand at a moment, with batch and expiry where
  recorded. & Needed only if distribution is in scope (Section~\ref{sec:open}). \\ \hline
Allocation Recommendation & A proposed transfer of stock: commodity, quantity, from and
  to, and the reason. & For a person to accept or reject; never automatic. \\ \hline
\rowcolor{rowshade}Shortage Event & A period when a facility ran out of a commodity, or
  fell below its reserve level. & The harm the study measures, if distribution is in scope
  (OD-3). \\ \hline
