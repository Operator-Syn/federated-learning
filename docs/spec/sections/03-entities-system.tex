\section{System entities}
\label{sec:entities-system}

A barangay health center exists whether or not anything is built around it. A round, the
facilities that joined it, and the audit record of what happened exist only while the
software is running. The entities below are defined in those terms, because they are how the
study's evidence is recorded: who trained, how long a round took, which facility never
answered.

\noindent A filled square marks the entities implemented by the first version.

Node\core & The facility's agent. Holds that facility's records and performs local
  training. & The only component that ever reads rows. \\ \hline
\rowcolor{rowshade}Aggregator\core & The coordinating server. Shares the global model,
  receives parameter updates, and records what happened. & Hosting location is an open
  decision (Section~\ref{sec:open}). \\ \hline
Round\core & One exchange: model distributed, local training at selected facilities,
  parameter updates aggregated. Identified by number; records which facilities were
  selected, which could be reached, which trained, the duration and the timestamps. & A round
  completes at the pace
  of its slowest participant (Section~\ref{sec:metrics}). \\ \hline
\rowcolor{rowshade}Model Version\core & A global model artifact: parameter vector,
  architecture and configuration hash, training provenance. & Parameters only. No row,
  label or gradient for an individual record is ever stored in a model version. \\ \hline
Selection Decision & The per-round record of which facilities were invited, which
  answered, and the policy that chose them. & The evidence for whatever a policy claims
  about inclusion. \\ \hline
\rowcolor{rowshade}Participation Record & Cumulative counts of rounds each facility
  contributed to. & Basis of the participation floor metric
  (Section~\ref{sec:metrics}). \\ \hline
Policy Configuration & The named selection family and its parameters for a run. & Names
  are fixed by Section~\ref{sec:policies} and used unchanged in configuration and
  reports. \\ \hline
\rowcolor{rowshade}Calibration Profile & The parameter set that describes a city: facility
  roster, reliabilities, latencies, volumes, floor and target. & The durable artifact the
  study intends to leave behind, so the comparison can be re-run as facilities come
  online. \\ \hline
Evaluation Snapshot & The held-out evaluation of a model version at a point in time:
  data version, metric values, seed. & Ties a reported number to the exact conditions
  that produced it. \\ \hline
\rowcolor{rowshade}Audit Record & Who performed which action on which object, when, under
  which configuration hash. & Required for conformance;
  see Section~\ref{sec:conformance}. \\ \hline
\end{spectab}
