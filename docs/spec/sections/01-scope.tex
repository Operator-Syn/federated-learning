\section{Scope and non-claims}
\label{sec:scope}

\subsection{Purpose}
The system predicts medicine demand at individual barangay health centers, without moving
patient records out of the facilities that hold them. Whether it also supports distribution,
by tracking stock positions and recommending allocation, is open decision OD-3 in
Section~\ref{sec:open}; the entities for it are defined so that the answer can change without
redesigning the record.
Federated learning supplies the mechanism: each facility trains on its own records and
only model parameters cross to an aggregator, the coordinating server.

\subsection{Two modes of use}
The same entity definitions serve both, because a simulated facility must be the same
\emph{kind} of thing as a real one:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.2em]
  \item[Simulated mode] Every entity is instantiated with synthetic values. This is the mode the
    thesis evaluates until a data-sharing agreement with the City Health Office is signed, and
    every artifact produced in it is labelled synthetic.
  \item[Deployment mode] Entities are instantiated from City Health Office (CHO) records under
    that agreement. It supersedes simulated mode for the pilot sites once the agreement exists;
    until then the study is a simulation, not a deployed service.
\end{description}

\subsection{What this specification does not claim}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.2em]
  \item No policy is better than another. Comparing them is the study's work.
  \item No number here is Iligan's until calibration is separately justified. While the study
    runs in simulated mode every reported value is synthetic and labelled so; values derived from
    CHO records take their provenance from the signed agreement, not from this document.
  \item The predictive model is not the contribution. A model that works is part of the
    study's goal, since every policy is measured against one, but it is specified only far
    enough to serve that measurement; improving it is not the aim.
  \item The design is not validated until a pilot runs. Simulated results establish that the
    prototype runs and the metrics compute; they do not establish that the system works in a
    health center, and nothing in this document should be read as an operational claim.
  \item Every reported value is admissible only with the seed and configuration that
    produced it.
  \item Nothing in this document constitutes legal terms. The governance requirements in
    Section~\ref{sec:governance} state what the system must conform to; the data-sharing
    agreement is drafted by the partner, not by the study.
\end{enumerate}
