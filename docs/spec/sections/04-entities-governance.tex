\section{Governance entities}
\label{sec:governance}

These are not software. They are the objects that make it lawful to process a real
clinic's data, and the system must conform to them; there is nothing here to implement. They
are specified here because two design decisions depend on them (the audit record and
the deletion path), and because retrofitting either one is a rewrite.

Data Controller & The body that determines the purpose of processing and carries the

  recorded per dataset version, not left implicit. \\ \hline
\rowcolor{rowshade}Data Processor & The party that processes on the controller's
  instruction, and only for the stated purpose. Expected to be the university. & Reuse for

Data-Sharing Agreement & The signed instrument covering data scope, purpose, security,
  retention, breach notification, publication rights and termination. & Fixes what may be

  required. & Approval reference is recorded per run; its scope bounds which evaluations
  are admissible. \\ \hline
Operator Role & The permission to start a run, read a model output, or export a report, held
  separately from server administration. & Requires role-based access and one audit record
  per action. \\ \hline
\rowcolor{rowshade}Retention Schedule & How long raw observations, model versions,
  evaluation snapshots and audit records are kept, and what deletes them. & Deletion must
  be an operation the system performs, not a sentence in a policy. \\ \hline

\subsection{The patient is deliberately absent}
There is no patient entity in this system: no identifier, no pseudonym, no row, no key.
Demand exists only as aggregate counts per facility, commodity and period. This is a
design claim, not a promise, and every downstream privacy statement depends on it
remaining true. Any future feature that would require patient-level data is out of scope
and requires a new specification.

\subsection{Transition boundary}
Until a data-sharing agreement and ethics clearance exist, every value the system
processes is synthetic and every artifact says so. This boundary is stated once here and
referenced wherever a result is reported.
